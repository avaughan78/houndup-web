2. What Hound-Up is

Hound-Up is a business management tool for solo dog walkers: scheduling, client and dog records, and invoicing. It works fully offline, and the core app — scheduling, records, and invoicing — is free, with no account required. iCloud sync, emailing invoices, reading a pasted booking message, publishing a calendar link, and contacting support are all optional features you can choose to use, also free and with no account required.

Hound-Up Pro is a separate, optional paid subscription — see Section 5 — that adds automatic background features (backup and multi-device sync, auto-nudge, instant booking and payment notifications) for walkers who want them, running via a real account.

4. Data processing terms

This section applies whenever a feature you use sends data through Hound-Up's backend — emailing an invoice, reading a pasted booking message, publishing a calendar feed, contacting support, or storing your business/client data via Cloud Sync — and sets out the terms on which that happens, as required by Article 28 UK GDPR.

Roles. You are the data controller for the personal data you enter about your clients and their dogs. Hound-Up (Andy Vaughan) acts as your data processor, but only for the specific piece of data a feature you've actively used needs to do its job — never for your records as a whole, unless you've turned on Cloud Sync (see below).

Subject matter, duration, and nature of processing. For a one-off action (relaying one invoice email, parsing one pasted message, hosting one calendar feed file), processing is limited to what that single action requires and lasts only as long as needed to complete it — nothing is retained by the backend beyond that, except the calendar feed file itself, which persists until you revoke or replace it. If you've turned on Cloud Sync, processing is ongoing instead: your business and client records are stored continuously for as long as Cloud Sync stays on, until you turn it off and delete the data (Settings → Cloud Sync → "Delete cloud data").

Categories of data and data subjects. For a one-off action: whatever you've chosen to include in the specific invoice, message, or feed being sent — typically your client's name, contact details, and dog's name — plus your own business details where relevant. For Cloud Sync: everything listed in the Privacy Policy's Cloud Sync section, including clients' medical/vet notes and your own bank details. The data subjects are your clients and their households, and you yourself (for your own business/bank details).

Sub-processors. The current list is published in the Privacy Policy. Andy Vaughan won't engage a new sub-processor, or replace an existing one, for these features without updating that list and this page first.

Security. Every request to the backend is authenticated per-device via Apple's App Attest (or your Cloud Sync account, if signed in), sent over TLS, and processed on infrastructure hosted in the EU (Supabase, eu-west-1) except where a sub-processor's own service (Resend, Microsoft Azure OpenAI, Stripe) requires otherwise for that specific task.

International transfers. Some sub-processors may process data outside the UK/EEA as part of delivering their service (for example, Microsoft's global Azure infrastructure, or Stripe's). Where that happens, it's under that provider's own standard contractual safeguards for international transfers.

Assistance with data subject requests. If one of your clients asks you to access, correct, or erase data that passed through Hound-Up's backend, Andy Vaughan will reasonably assist. For a one-off action, there's usually nothing left on the backend to act on beyond an active calendar feed file. If you've turned on Cloud Sync, your client's data may still be stored there until you delete it yourself, or ask us to.

5. Hound-Up Pro (subscription)

Hound-Up Pro is an optional auto-renewing subscription, currently £9.99/month (or your local currency equivalent), billed through your Apple ID. Subscribing turns on Cloud Sync: automatic backup and multi-device sync, auto-nudge for overdue invoices even with the app closed, an automatically-updated availability calendar and calendar feed, and instant booking-request and invoice-paid notifications.

Payment is charged to your Apple ID when you confirm purchase. The subscription renews automatically unless you turn off auto-renewal at least 24 hours before the end of the current period; your Apple ID will be charged for renewal within 24 hours before that period ends, at the price then in effect. Manage or cancel anytime in your device's Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions, or via Settings → Cloud Sync → "Manage subscription" in the app, which opens the same place. Cancelling stops future billing at the end of the current period; it doesn't refund the current period or retroactively delete data already stored (see Section 4 and the Privacy Policy for how to do that separately).

Apple, not Hound-Up, processes this payment and holds your payment details — see Apple's own terms at apple.com/legal/internet-services/itunes/.
