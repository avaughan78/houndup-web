What's inside

Everything a solo walker's day actually needs

Client & dog onboarding

A profile for every dog — breed, age, temperament, diet, medical notes — plus emergency contacts, vet details, and entry instructions like lockbox or alarm codes.

  • Per-owner custom hourly rates
  • Safe, tailored care at a glance before you knock

Scheduling & planning

Set up recurring patterns — every Monday, Wednesday, Friday — once, or drop in an ad-hoc "could you walk Buster on Thursday?" in seconds.

  • A clean, native calendar view of your week
  • See every future booking at a glance

Walk logging

Log a walk before you go or after the fact — solo or as a group — with 30 or 60-minute billing built right into the record.

  • Notes captured by typing or speaking
  • No timer to forget to start

Finance & invoicing

Every completed walk rolls straight into a ledger, grouped by owner. Review it any time — nothing to type up at the end of the month.

  • One tap to send all via email or WhatsApp
  • Mark paid to archive it out of your day-to-day view

Getting paid

From walk to paid, without opening a spreadsheet

  1. 1

    Auto-compiled

    Every completed walk adds its line item to the client's ledger by itself.

  2. 2

    One-tap batch send

    At the end of the week or month, send every invoice at once via email or WhatsApp.

  3. 3

    Zero-app owner payment

    Owners get a link with UK bank details or a pay link — no app to download.

  4. 4

    Reconciliation

    Mark invoices paid as the money lands, and they're out of your day-to-day view.
