Getting paid

From walk to paid, without opening a spreadsheet

  1. 1

    Auto-compiled

    Every completed walk adds its line item to the client's ledger by itself.

  2. 2

    One-tap batch send

    At the end of the week or month, send every invoice at once via email or WhatsApp.

  3. 3

    Simple payments

    Clients get sent a link with your UK bank details, or you can use Stripe to send them an instant payment link* — no app to download.

  4. 4

    Reconciliation

    Mark invoices paid as the money lands, and they're out of your day-to-day view.

*Stripe charge a small commission if you choose to use their service — it can make paying you easier and quicker for your clients. Hound-Up doesn't receive or charge anything for this integration, and once a client pays via Stripe, the invoice is automatically marked as paid.
