🐕 Client & Dog Records

  • Full profile per dog — breed, sex, age (or birthdate), temperament, diet notes
  • Medical notes with a flagged MEDICAL badge so nothing urgent gets missed
  • Vet name, phone and address on file for every dog
  • Emergency contacts and home-entry instructions (lockbox codes, alarm codes) stored per client
  • Custom hourly rates per client — override your defaults for anyone, without fuss
  • Dog photos and colour-tagged avatars throughout the app

📅 Scheduling & Calendar

  • Recurring schedules (e.g. every Mon/Wed/Fri, or alternate-week fortnightly patterns)
  • One-off ad-hoc bookings in seconds
  • Clean native month/week calendar view
  • House sits built in as a first-class booking type alongside walks — hourly or multi-night, billed correctly either way
  • Subscribeable calendar feed — pipe your bookings straight into Apple/Google Calendar
  • Publish a public availability page so clients can see your free slots before they ask Pro

🚶 Walk Logging & Live Tracking

  • Log a walk before you go, or after the fact — no rigid timers required
  • 30 or 60-minute walks billed automatically at the right rate
  • Group walks with multiple dogs in one entry
  • Live GPS route tracking with real-time distance and duration
  • Dynamic Island & Lock Screen live activity — see your walk running right from the lock screen, with a one-tap "Stop & Save"
  • Voice-to-text walk notes, transcribed entirely on-device — nothing ever leaves the phone
  • Route map saved against every completed walk

📩 Smart Booking Import

  • Paste a client's WhatsApp/text message straight in — AI extracts who, when, and what's needed
  • Understands new bookings, recurring requests, and edits/cancellations to existing bookings
  • Auto-matches the dog mentioned to your existing records
  • Fills in fuzzy dates ("next Tuesday afternoon"), durations ("an hour's walk") and weekday patterns automatically
  • You review and confirm everything before it's saved — always in control

💷 Invoicing & Finance

  • Every completed walk/sit rolls automatically into a per-client ledger
  • Professional PDF invoices with your logo, business name and bank details
  • Send invoices by email or WhatsApp in one tap
  • Mark invoices paid and archive them out of your daily view
  • Manual payment reminders (nudges) for anything overdue
  • Monthly invoicing reminder notification, set to whatever day suits you
  • Accountant-ready CSV export by UK tax year (6 April–5 April, HMRC's own convention) — hand it straight to your accountant
  • Accept card and Apple Pay via Stripe payment links, with automatic "paid" detection Pro
  • Auto-nudge overdue invoices even with the app closed Pro
  • Automatic post-walk visit summary emails to owners Pro

🔒 Privacy & Reliability

  • Optional iCloud sync across your own devices, entirely between you and Apple
