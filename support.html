Common questions

Does Hound-Up need an internet connection?

No — scheduling, client and dog records, walks, and invoicing all work fully offline. An internet connection is only used if you turn on iCloud sync, or when you choose to email an invoice.

Do I need to create an account?

No. There's no sign-in — your data lives on your device, and optionally in your own private iCloud account if you turn on sync in Settings.

Where does my data go?

See the Privacy Policy for the full picture — in short, everything stays on your device (and your own iCloud, if enabled), except the specific invoice you choose to email, which is relayed through a backend purely to deliver that one message.
